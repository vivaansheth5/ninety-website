What is Impulsify?

Impulsify is an Indian online store for unusual, clever and beautifully designed gadgets — the kind of thing you didn't know you needed until you saw it.

Are the prices final?

Not yet. This is a preview of the store and prices are indicative. Final selling prices will be confirmed before launch and shown at checkout.

How does checkout work?

Checkout will run on Shopify's secure hosted checkout, which supports guest checkout — no account needed. In this preview, checkout is in demo mode and no payment is taken.

Do you ship across India?

That is the plan. Shipping rates, delivery timelines and serviceable pin codes will be published in our shipping policy before launch.

Why do some specs say 'pending supplier confirmation'?

We only publish specifications we have verified. Where we're still confirming details with our supplier, we say so instead of guessing.
